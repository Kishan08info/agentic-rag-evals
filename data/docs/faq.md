# Nimbus Analytics — FAQ

## How do I reset my password?

Click "Forgot password" on the login page and enter your account email. You will
receive a reset link valid for 60 minutes. If it expires, request a new one —
old links are invalidated automatically.

## Can I export my data?

Yes. Any Analyst or Admin can export dashboard results to **CSV** from the
dashboard menu. Raw event exports to your warehouse run nightly on Scale plans
and above. One-off historical backfills are available on Enterprise.

## How do I delete my workspace?

Go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete workspace. Deletion is permanent and
erases all events, dashboards, and API keys within 30 days. This action requires
typing the workspace name to confirm.

## What support options exist?

- Starter: community forum and help center.
- Growth: email support, 24-hour response target on business days.
- Scale and Enterprise: priority email plus a shared Slack channel, with a
  4-hour response target.

## Where is the status page?

Live service status is published at `status.nimbus.example.com`, including
90 days of uptime history and incident postmortems.
