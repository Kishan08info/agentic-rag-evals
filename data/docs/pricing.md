# Nimbus Analytics — Pricing

Nimbus Analytics offers four plans. All prices are in USD and all plans include
unlimited seats and unlimited saved dashboards.

## Plans

- **Starter — Free.** Up to 10,000 tracked events per month, 30-day data retention,
  community support.
- **Growth — $49 per month.** Up to 1,000,000 tracked events per month, 13-month
  data retention, funnels and cohorts, email support with a 24-hour response target.
- **Scale — $249 per month.** Up to 10,000,000 tracked events per month, 13-month
  data retention, warehouse export, SSO/SAML, a dedicated success manager.
- **Enterprise — Custom pricing.** Unlimited events, custom data-retention terms,
  EU data residency, custom security review, 99.99% uptime SLA.

## Billing details

- Annual billing saves 20% compared to monthly billing on Growth and Scale.
- Overage events are billed at $5 per additional 100,000 events on paid plans.
- You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from the billing page; changes
  take effect at the start of the next billing cycle and there are no cancellation fees.
- Nimbus does not charge per seat. Every plan includes unlimited team members.
